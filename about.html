Meet the Artisans

Ndop & Co. is a student project store that plans to buy finished goods in small batches from partner artisans and ship orders within the United States. The profiles below are sample portraits written for this course project. They describe the kinds of craft the shop will feature: indigo ndop cloth, raffia weaving, and colorful embroidery.

AN

Amina N.

Craft: Indigo ndop cloth

Amina dyes cotton with indigo and stamps geometric resist patterns inspired by Grassfields ndop cloth. Her table runners and throws are the pieces that gave the store its name.

JT

Joseph T.

Craft: Raffia baskets

Joseph weaves storage baskets and nested bowls from raffia. Tight, even coils make the baskets sturdy enough for everyday use, and each lid is finished by hand.

EM

Esther M.

Craft: Embroidery

Esther embroidered the red, gold, and green border on the toghu-inspired tote. She also stitches the bands on scarves and head wraps in the apparel collection.
